About Us

More Than Just a Book Club.

BookClubEthiopia is a student-led reading community created to bring students together through the power of books. We believe reading is more than finishing a book — it is about discovering new perspectives, having meaningful conversations, thinking critically, and connecting with people who share the same curiosity.

Our goal is simple: to build a community where students across Ethiopia can read, discuss, discover, and grow together.

Open to students from any school in Ethiopia
